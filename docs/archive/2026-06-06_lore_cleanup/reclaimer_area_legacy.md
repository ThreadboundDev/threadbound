# The Reclaimer’s Domain (Secret Area)

---

## 🧵 Overview

A hidden region unlocked only through **extreme alignment**:

- Absorb all Thread Masters  
- OR  
- Spare all Thread Masters  

The player is **pulled into this domain against their will**.

This is not a reward.

It is a reckoning.

---

## 🐛 The Reclaimers

Ancient, non-humanoid beings.

- Insect-like (silkworm / woven creatures)  
- Composed of layered, living threads  
- Move with unnatural precision and purpose  

They exist to:

> **Restore threads to their natural state**

---

### Perception of the Player

The Reclaimers see the Threadborne as:

- A mistake  
- A stolen fragment  
- A disruption of the natural order  

They do not speak.

They communicate through:
- motion  
- pressure  
- intent  

---

## ⚖️ The Draining

Upon entering the domain:

- All color is removed  
- All absorbed abilities are stripped  
- All equipment becomes **dormant**  

The player is reduced to:

> **Their base form — fragile, incomplete**

---

### Mechanical Shift

- From power → vulnerability  
- From expression → survival  
- From mastery → fundamentals  

This forces the player to rely on:

- core movement  
- timing  
- understanding of systems  

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Identity

This area is intentionally different from the rest of the game.

### Pace
- Slower  
- Heavier  
- More deliberate  

### Atmosphere
- Minimal sound  
- Hollow ambience  
- Near silence  

### Emotion
- Isolation  
- Reflection  
- Unease  

---

## 🔧 The Loom Blade (Reforging)

Before confronting the Reclaimer:

The player may reforge the **Loom Blade**.

---

### Requirements

- Base weapon (starting piece)  
- 3 fragments (Red, Blue, Yellow regions)  

---

### Result

A unified weapon representing:

> **The restored whole of the Tapestry**

---

### Purpose

- Mechanical advantage in the upcoming fight  
- Symbolic reconstruction of identity  

---

## ⚔️ The Reclaimer (Boss)

A primordial being of reclamation.

---

### Nature

- Not a ruler  
- Not a guide  
- A force of correction  

---

### Motivation

- Return all threads to their rightful place  
- Undo deviation from natural order  
- Erase what should not exist  

---

### Behavior

- Manipulative  
- Persuasive  
- Philosophical without words  

It does not oppose the player with anger—

but with certainty.

---

## 🧠 Philosophy

> “Threads should not belong to anyone.”

To the Reclaimer:

- Absorption is corruption  
- Sparing is avoidance  

Both are violations.

---

## 🔁 The Choice

After defeating the Reclaimer:

The player must choose again.

---

### If the Player is CONSISTENT

- Absorb all → Absorb the Reclaimer  
- Spare all → Spare the Reclaimer  

---

#### Result

- Identity is preserved  
- The player moves forward  
- Unlocks:
  - Final Weaver path  
  - Secret post-Weaver encounter  

---

### If the Player is INCONSISTENT

The Reclaimer betrays the player.

---

#### Outcome

- Remaining life force is drained  
- Immediate death ending  

---

### Meaning

> The Reclaimer punishes contradiction.

---

## 🧩 Narrative Role

The Reclaimer is a counterpoint to the Weaver.

- The Weaver broke the system  
- The Reclaimer attempts to **erase interference**  

It does not ask what the world should become.

It asks:

> Should it be changed at all?

---

## 🎯 Design Purpose

The Reclaimer’s Domain exists to:

1. Strip the player of power  
2. Force reflection on choices  
3. Test mechanical fundamentals  
4. Reinforce commitment to a path  
5. Gate the game’s deepest outcomes  

---

## 🔥 Core Theme

> You cannot escape what you’ve become.

The Reclaimer does not judge your strength.

It judges your **consistency**.
