\# Symbolism \& Motifs



The game shows themes through environment, dialogue, and mechanics — never preaches.



\- \*\*Tapestry\*\* = Predestination / divine plan

\- \*\*Shattering the Loom\*\* = Rebellion against fate (Luciferian free will echoes)

\- \*\*Absorption\*\* = Corruption through power (visual fraying, darker palettes)

\- \*\*Sparing\*\* = Grace, mercy, redemption (allies, hopeful echoes)

\- \*\*Reclaimers\*\* = Legalism / blind obedience

\- \*\*Mysterious Follower\*\* = Conscience / tempter / prophet figure

\- \*\*Weaving yourself\*\* = Becoming creator — or destroying the need for one



Motifs: frayed cloth, glowing threads, stained-glass memories, silk flows.
