\# Lore \& World of Eryndor



Eryndor is a world once defined by order.



Now, it exists in fragments.



\---



\## 🧵 The Grand Tapestry



Long ago, reality was shaped by the \*\*Grand Tapestry\*\*—



a living weave that dictated the flow of existence.



Every life, every event, every outcome  

was part of a pattern maintained at the \*\*Grand Loom\*\*.



At its center stood the \*\*Weaver\*\*.



\---



\## 🧶 The Primordial Threads



Three fundamental threads formed the foundation of the Tapestry:



\- 🟥 \*\*Thread of Power\*\*  

&#x20; Force, will, and domination  



\- 🟦 \*\*Thread of Balance\*\*  

&#x20; Harmony, restraint, and continuity  



\- 🟨 \*\*Thread of Essence\*\*  

&#x20; Life, memory, and perception  



These threads did not simply influence the world—



They \*\*defined it\*\*.



\---



\## ⚖️ The Breaking of the Loom



At some point, the Weaver made a choice.



They shattered the Loom.



Whether this act was:



\- rebellion  

\- liberation  

\- or a mistake  



is never fully known.



\---



\### The Result



\- The Tapestry unraveled  

\- Destiny fractured  

\- The world lost cohesion  



Without the Loom:



\- fate no longer holds  

\- meaning is no longer fixed  

\- identity is no longer assigned  



\---



\## 🌍 The World After



Eryndor did not end.



It \*\*changed\*\*.



Fragments of the old system remain:



\- regions shaped by dominant threads  

\- beings still bound to singular purpose  

\- remnants of structure within chaos  



But nothing is whole anymore.



\---



\## 👑 The Thread Masters



In the absence of the Weaver’s control, three figures now hold the primordial threads:



\- The \*\*Monarch\*\* (Power)  

\- The \*\*Hermit\*\* (Balance)  

\- The \*\*Sage\*\* (Essence)  



Each has taken their thread and shaped a domain around it.



They are not rulers by design—



They are \*\*products of what they hold\*\*.



\---



\## 🐛 The Reclaimers



Strange beings known as the \*\*Reclaimers\*\* move through the broken world.



They:



\- gather loose threads  

\- consume or reclaim fragments of the Tapestry  

\- attempt to restore a form of natural order  



Their purpose is unclear.



They may be:



\- remnants of the old system  

\- a corrective force  

\- or something entirely separate  



\---



\## 🧵 The Threadborne



From the moment the Loom shattered, one thread fell free.



That thread became \*\*the Threadborne\*\*.



Unlike all others:



\- it is not bound to a single purpose  

\- it does not belong to a fixed pattern  

\- it can take, reject, or reshape threads  



It is:



\- anomaly  

\- possibility  

\- disruption  



\---



\## 🔁 A World Without Answers



Eryndor no longer has a single truth.



Instead, it exists in tension between:



\- control and freedom  

\- structure and chaos  

\- meaning and interpretation  



No force in the world fully understands what it has become.



\---



\## 🎭 The Player’s Role



The Threadborne does not restore the world.



They do not destroy it.



They \*\*define it\*\*.



Through their journey, the player determines:



\- what power means  

\- what balance requires  

\- what truth becomes  



\---



\## 🔥 Core Question



The world of Eryndor is not asking:



> “What is the correct path?”



It is asking:



> “What happens when nothing decides for you?”



\---



\## 🧠 Summary



Eryndor is a world that has lost its structure.



What remains is:



\- fragments of purpose  

\- echoes of control  

\- and the possibility of something new  



\---



> The Tapestry is gone.



What replaces it…  

is up to you.
