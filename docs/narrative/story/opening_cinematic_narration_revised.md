# Opening Cinematic Narration (Revised)

There was a time when the world was... simpler.

At least, I think it was.

Life was young then.

Small things wandered the forests and fields. They ate, slept, fought, and fled. Seasons came and went. Rivers carved their paths. The stars turned overhead.

And I watched.

Though, I did not know that was what I was doing.

Watching.

I did not know many things then.

I did not know what I was.

Or why I was.

I only remember a feeling.

A question.

One that seemed to linger longer each time it appeared.

Can a world wonder?

Can I?

I don't know how long I carried that question.

Long enough, perhaps.

Because eventually, the wondering became something more.

A thought.

No... Thought.

He was a part of me, I think.

Or perhaps I was a part of him.

It's difficult to say where one thing ends and another begins.

Especially after so much time.

What I do remember is that he looked upon the world and saw possibility everywhere.

Where I saw rivers, he saw journeys.

Where I saw mountains, he saw horizons.

Where I saw life, he saw stories.

And he wanted more of them.

So together, we wove.

Not because anything was missing.

Not at first.

But because creation is a strange thing.

Once it begins, it seems to want to continue.

The world grew.

Life grew with it.

And for a while, that was enough.

But Thought was curious.

Perhaps too curious.

Then again...

most wonderful things begin that way.

He wished to know the world he had helped shape.

Not from above.

But from within.

So he went to walk among it.

And the ages passed.

More lives.

More stories.

More questions.

Always questions.

Eventually, others began asking them too.

A King.

A Monk.

A Scholar.

They searched for the source of the world around them.

For the place where its wonders began.

And after much searching...

they found it.

I remember being afraid.

Though I did not understand why.

Not until later.

The keepers failed.

The Threads were taken.

And something changed.

Not all at once.

More like a crack spreading through stone.

Slow.

Quiet.

Easy to miss.

Until suddenly it wasn't.

What had once moved together began to pull apart.

The world endured.

It always does.

But the wound remained.

The King.

The Monk.

The Scholar.

They saw what the Threads were making of them.

And fearing what they might become, they sought another answer.

A keeper.

One who could mend what was fraying.

One who could hold together what others could not.

The Weaver.

For a time, that answer seemed enough.

But time changes everything.

Even good intentions.

Especially good intentions.

And now...

the Weaver still labors.

Still gathers.

Still binds.

Still tries to hold the world together.

Yet somewhere amid the countless threads...

one slipped free.

A small thing, perhaps.

Easy to overlook.

But then...

once, a question became Thought.

I wonder what a loose thread could become.
