\# Opening Cinematic – Threadbound



\## The Grand Tapestry



The game begins in silence.



A vast, living tapestry stretches endlessly across the screen — intricate, shifting, alive.



Threads of \*\*Red (Power)\*\*, \*\*Blue (Balance)\*\*, and \*\*Yellow (Essence)\*\* weave together to form the history — or perhaps the destiny — of the world.



It is unclear:



\- Is this recording what has happened?

\- Or is it deciding what will happen?



\## The Weaver



At the center of it all sits \*\*The Weaver\*\*, tirelessly working at the \*\*Grand Loom\*\*.



A towering, many-limbed figure — neither fully divine nor mortal.



They weave endlessly.



Without pause.



Without rest.



Until something changes.



\## The Breaking



The Weaver hesitates.



Something is wrong.



Frustration builds — subtle at first, then overwhelming.



And suddenly—



\*\*They tear the tapestry apart.\*\*



Threads snap.



Color fractures.



Reality itself begins to unravel.



The Grand Loom shatters.



\## The Last Thread



From the destruction, one final thread falls.



Alone.



Unclaimed.



It drifts downward into darkness — into a fracture in the world.



As it falls…



…it begins to take shape.



\## The Threadborne



The thread lands.



And becomes \*\*you\*\*.



A fragile, incomplete being.



A remnant of something greater.



A possibility.



\## The Chamber of the First Weave



You awaken in a quiet, sacred place:



\*\*The Chamber of the First Weave\*\*



Here lie the origins of all threads:



\- The Thread of Power (Red)

\- The Thread of Balance (Blue)

\- The Thread of Essence (Yellow)



But something is wrong.



They are gone.



Taken.



\## The Call Forward



You learn:



Three beings — the \*\*Thread Masters\*\* — now hold these primordial threads.



To return to the Grand Loom…



To confront the Weaver…



You must reclaim them.



Your journey begins.
