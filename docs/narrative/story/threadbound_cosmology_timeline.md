# Cosmology Timeline – Threadbound

## 1. Eryndor Exists

Eryndor is the living world-consciousness.

She is ancient, aware, and alive.

She is not omnipotent.

She does not impose control over creation.

---

## 2. Thought Emerges

From Eryndor, Thought becomes self-aware.

Thought is:

- a fragment of Eryndor
- the first awareness
- the First Weaver

He is young on a cosmic scale.

He desires companionship.

---

## 3. The World Is Woven

Thought, Eryndor, and the primordial Threads weave the world together.

The Threads are:

- Power
- Balance
- Essence

They are not sentient.

They are forces.

They are meant to remain united and diffused through Eryndor.

---

## 4. Thought Enters Creation

Thought does not want to merely observe the world.

He wants to be part of it.

He enters creation willingly.

He leaves the Threads unattended, not through malice, but through innocence and trust.

He cannot yet imagine that anyone would seek to possess them.

---

## 5. The Reclaimers Are Uplifted

As Thought's final great work, he uplifts native creatures of Eryndor into sentience.

These become the Reclaimers.

They are given society, memory, and sacred purpose.

Their duty:

> Protect the Threads so that no one meddles with choice.

---

## 6. Civilizations Study Magic

Over long ages, the peoples of Eryndor study the world's magic.

They discover that magic flows through reality without depletion.

They conclude that there must be a primordial source.

---

## 7. The Three Discover the Threads

Three mortals discover the Threads:

- a ruler/protector
- a contemplative mediator
- a scholar/seeker

They do not begin as villains.

They believe they can understand or steward the source.

---

## 8. The Threads Are Taken

The Three take the Threads.

They become immortal.

The Threads are separated from:

- each other
- Eryndor
- their natural harmony

This causes a wound in creation.

---

## 9. The Reclaimers Fail

The Reclaimers fail to prevent the separation.

This failure becomes their defining trauma.

Their original purpose hardens into obsession.

---

## 10. The Three Create the Weaver

Before they are fully consumed by the Threads, the Three realize they cannot trust themselves.

Together, in a moment of clarity, they create the Weaver.

The Weaver is meant to be a neutral steward.

It exists to govern what the Three can no longer govern.

---

## 11. The Thread Masters Become Consumed

Over centuries or millennia, the Three become more tightly fused with their Threads.

They do not simply hold power.

They become manifestations of isolated principles.

- Monarch becomes Power without restraint
- Hermit becomes Balance without action
- Sage becomes Essence without grounding

They can technically relinquish the Threads, but doing so would mean mortality, loss, withdrawal, and likely death.

They are addicted in a mythic sense.

---

## 12. The Weaver Turns Toward Control

The Weaver tries to stabilize the world.

Over time, it concludes that choice is the source of catastrophe.

It begins to manage freedom.

Then restrict it.

Then control it.

---

## 13. Eryndor Responds

Eryndor feels the wound, but cannot simply override free will.

The Threads strain toward reunion.

Thought recognizes the failure of freedom without guidance.

Together, instinctively or mysteriously, they produce one loose Thread.

---

## 14. The Threadborne Awakens

The Threadborne is:

- untuned
- unclaimed
- untainted
- unbound

They are not a chosen hero.

They are possibility.

They are the first being capable of answering the wound without inheriting a fixed answer.

---

## 15. The Game Begins

The Threadborne awakens into a fractured world.

The central question is no longer:

> Can the world be saved?

It is:

> What should freedom become?
