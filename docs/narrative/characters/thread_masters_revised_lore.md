# Thread Masters & Separation – Revised Lore

## Before the Threads

The Thread Masters were once mortal.

They were not tyrants or villains.

They were seekers.

Each represented a different lens through which reality was understood.

Together, they discovered the primordial source.

---

## The Monarch

Before becoming Monarch:

- protector
- ruler
- warrior

He viewed power as responsibility.

His desire was protection and order.

After receiving the Thread of Power:

he became immortal.

But over centuries:

Power separated from restraint and wisdom becomes domination.

His virtue collapses into excess.

Eventually:

Power becomes something to seize and maintain.

---

## The Hermit

Before becoming Hermit:

- monk
- contemplative
- mediator

He valued restraint and harmony.

After receiving the Thread of Balance:

he becomes immortal.

But Balance isolated from compassion becomes paralysis.

Over ages:

hesitation becomes identity.

He withdraws from the world seeking equilibrium.

Eventually:

action itself becomes suspect.

---

## The Sage

Before becoming Sage:

- scholar
- philosopher
- seeker of truth

She pursued knowledge sincerely.

After receiving the Thread of Essence:

she becomes immortal.

But Essence separated from grounding becomes endless searching.

Answers no longer satisfy.

Truth becomes infinitely recursive.

Eventually:

she seeks not truth—

but endless understanding.

---

## Core Principle

The Threads do not corrupt through evil intent.

They distort through isolation.

They were never meant to exist apart from:

- Eryndor
- one another
- or the harmony of creation.

The tragedy of the Thread Masters is not wickedness.

It is virtue transformed into absolutes.
