\# The Mysterious Follower



A recurring figure who appears throughout the Threadborne’s journey — never fully ally, never fully enemy.



They watch. They question. They intervene.



And they always seem to know more than they should.



\---



\## 🧵 Role in the Game



The Mysterious Follower serves as:



\- A \*\*narrative mirror\*\* to the player  

\- A \*\*philosophical antagonist/ally\*\* depending on choices  

\- A \*\*guide that never gives straight answers\*\*  

\- A \*\*presence that grows more personal over time\*\*



They appear at key moments:



\- After major traversal milestones  

\- Before or after Thread Master encounters  

\- In hidden or liminal spaces between regions  



They are not bound to the world in the same way other characters are.



\---



\## 🎭 Personality



\- Sharp, observant, and often unsettling  

\- Speaks in layered meaning rather than direct explanation  

\- Alternates between:

&#x20; - teasing

&#x20; - warning

&#x20; - quiet sincerity  



They never fully commit to helping or hindering you.



Instead, they \*\*react to who you are becoming\*\*.



\---



\## 🧠 Narrative Function



The Follower represents:



> The tension between \*\*choice and control\*\*



They constantly probe the player’s decisions:



\- Why take power?  

\- Why refuse it?  

\- Are your choices truly yours?  



As the game progresses, their dialogue shifts based on:



\- Absorbed threads  

\- Spared Thread Masters  

\- Player progression and behavior  



They become more:



\- \*\*Critical\*\* of domination (absorb-heavy paths)  

\- \*\*Concerned or curious\*\* in mercy paths  

\- \*\*Confrontational\*\* in extreme alignments  



\---



\## 🧶 Combat Presence



The Mysterious Follower is not initially hostile.



However:



\- They demonstrate advanced movement and weaving techniques

\- They mirror player capabilities in subtle ways

\- Their presence foreshadows a much deeper mastery of threads



\---



\## ⚔️ Secret Encounters



Under extreme conditions, the Follower becomes a boss.



\### Trigger Conditions



\- \*\*Spare All Thread Masters\*\* \*(pure white path)\*  

\- \*\*Absorb All Thread Masters\*\* \*(void/black path)\*  



\### Fight Concept



\- A \*\*mirror-style encounter\*\*

\- Uses variations of:

&#x20; - grapples

&#x20; - vaults

&#x20; - thread constructs

\- Adapts to player tendencies



This fight represents:



\- Judgment  

\- Reflection  

\- A final test of identity  



\---



\## 🧩 Implied Origin (Hidden Layer)



The Follower’s true nature is not explicitly stated early.



Clues suggest:



\- A deep connection to the Loom  

\- Knowledge of systems no longer accessible  

\- Familiarity with the act of weaving itself  



Their dialogue gradually shifts from observation…  

to recognition.



\---



\## 🎨 Visual Identity



\- Silhouette: slender, controlled, composed  

\- Outfit: refined but worn — suggesting both mastery and distance  

\- Weapon: a \*\*weaving staff / loom-harp hybrid\*\*

&#x20; - used for both combat and thread manipulation  



Their color presence subtly reacts to the player:



\- reflecting

\- contrasting

\- or resisting your palette



\---



\## 🧵 Design Notes



\- Never over-explain this character  

\- Dialogue should invite interpretation, not provide answers  

\- Encounters should feel intentional, not random  

\- The player should always feel:

&#x20; 

&#x20; > “They know something I don’t.”



\---



\## 🔥 Core Theme



> The Mysterious Follower is not here to guide you.



They are here to ask:



\*\*“What are you becoming?”\*\*


## Hidden Truth (Development Reference)

The Mysterious Follower is Thought.

The First Weaver.

A fragment of Eryndor who became self-aware during the world's earliest age.

After helping weave creation, Thought willingly entered the world to experience it directly.

He does not guide the Threadborne toward a specific outcome.

Instead, he observes.

Questions.

Challenges.

Over ages he has matured beyond his youthful belief that freedom alone is enough.

Yet he still refuses to rule.

He believes wisdom should guide choice.

Not replace it.

The player should never learn his identity through normal play.

The revelation that the Follower is Thought should be reserved for secret-path content and hidden endings.
