\# Thread Masters



The Thread Masters are the current bearers of the three primordial threads of the shattered Loom.



Each represents a fundamental aspect of existence:



\- \*\*Power (Red)\*\* — force, dominance, momentum  

\- \*\*Balance (Blue)\*\* — flow, restraint, harmony  

\- \*\*Essence (Yellow)\*\* — perception, interpretation, reality  



They are not merely bosses.



They are \*\*ideologies made manifest\*\*.



\---



\## 🧵 Overview



| Master      | Aspect            | Archetype            | Region                          | Core Theme                  |

|-------------|------------------|---------------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------------|

| \*\*Hermit\*\*  | Blue – Balance   | Isolated Guardian   | Flooded caverns, coastal temples| Letting go vs holding on    |

| \*\*Monarch\*\* | Red – Power      | Tyrant King         | War-forged cathedrals, molten thrones | Control vs domination       |

| \*\*Sage\*\*    | Yellow – Essence | Obsessive Scholar   | Endless libraries, golden mist  | Truth vs perception         |



\---



\## 🔴 The Monarch — Thread of Power



\*\*Archetype:\*\* Tyrannical ruler  

\*\*Domain:\*\* War-forged cathedrals, molten thrones, collapsing empires  



\### Personality

\- Regal, commanding, absolute  

\- Believes strength justifies rule  

\- Sees the Threadborne as either a successor… or a threat  



\### Philosophy

> Power must be taken — or it is meaningless.



\### Combat Identity

\- Heavy, momentum-driven attacks  

\- Space control and pressure  

\- Forces the player into reactive movement  



\### Outcomes



\*\*Spare\*\*

\- An heir rises to rebuild differently  

\- The world shows signs of resilience and renewal  

\- Power becomes something that can be \*\*shared\*\*



\*\*Absorb\*\*

\- You inherit raw destructive force  

\- The world becomes fearful, oppressive  

\- Power becomes something that is \*\*taken\*\*



\---



\## 🔵 The Hermit — Thread of Balance



\*\*Archetype:\*\* Isolated guardian  

\*\*Domain:\*\* Flooded caverns, tidal ruins, silent temples  



\### Personality

\- Calm, distant, introspective  

\- Protective, but withdrawn from the world  

\- Speaks in quiet truths rather than declarations  



\### Philosophy

> Balance is not maintained through action, but through restraint.



\### Combat Identity

\- Flowing movement and spacing  

\- Reactive patterns and counterplay  

\- Encourages patience and rhythm  



\### Outcomes



\*\*Spare\*\*

\- Their partner or successor continues their philosophy  

\- The world becomes warmer, more interconnected  

\- Balance becomes something that \*\*lives on\*\*



\*\*Absorb\*\*

\- You gain precise, fluid control over movement  

\- The world grows colder and more distant  

\- Balance becomes something that is \*\*controlled\*\*



\---



\## 🟡 The Sage — Thread of Essence



\*\*Archetype:\*\* Obsessive scholar  

\*\*Domain:\*\* Infinite libraries, shifting halls, golden haze  



\### Personality

\- Soft-spoken, curious, unsettling  

\- Speaks in riddles and layered meaning  

\- Seeks understanding beyond truth  



\### Philosophy

> Reality is not fixed — it is interpreted.



\### Combat Identity

\- Unpredictable, deceptive patterns  

\- Illusions, misdirection, spatial manipulation  

\- Forces the player to question what is real  



\### Outcomes



\*\*Spare\*\*

\- A student inherits their work (or challenges it)  

\- The world becomes more open to interpretation  

\- Truth becomes something that is \*\*explored\*\*



\*\*Absorb\*\*

\- You gain reality-bending abilities  

\- The world becomes unstable, uncertain  

\- Truth becomes something that is \*\*defined by you\*\*



\---



\## 🌍 World \& Visual Identity



Each Thread Master reshapes their region through their aspect:



\- \*\*Red (Monarch)\*\*  

&#x20; - Cracking stone, burning structures, violent motion  

&#x20; - Visual language: sharp, heavy, explosive  



\- \*\*Blue (Hermit)\*\*  

&#x20; - Water, reflection, suspended motion  

&#x20; - Visual language: flowing, soft, continuous  



\- \*\*Yellow (Sage)\*\*  

&#x20; - Shifting geometry, illusions, layered spaces  

&#x20; - Visual language: hazy, recursive, surreal  



\---



\## ⚖️ Design Role



The Thread Masters serve as:



\- \*\*Major gameplay milestones\*\*  

\- \*\*Sources of equipment (Gloves / Boots / Chest-Head)\*\*  

\- \*\*Narrative anchors for player choice\*\*  



Each encounter asks the player:



> Do you take what they have…  

> or allow it to remain?



\---



\## 🧵 Relationship to the Player



The Threadborne is not destined to defeat them.



They are:



\- a disruption  

\- a successor  

\- or a correction  



Depending on the path chosen.



\---



\## 🔥 Core Theme



> The Thread Masters are not villains.



They are \*\*answers\*\* to the same question:



\*\*What should be done with power?\*\*
