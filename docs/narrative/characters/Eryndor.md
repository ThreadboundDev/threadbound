\# Eryndor – Character Guide



\## Core Identity



Eryndor is the living consciousness of the world.



She is not a goddess in the traditional sense.



She is the world remembering itself.



\---



\## Personality



Eryndor is:



\* curious

\* reflective

\* compassionate

\* hopeful

\* occasionally melancholy



She is not detached.



She cares deeply about what happens within creation.



\---



\## Narrative Voice



Eryndor speaks like someone remembering.



Not someone lecturing.



She should sound:



\* personal

\* conversational

\* uncertain when appropriate



She may correct herself.



She may wonder aloud.



She does not speak with absolute certainty.



\---



\## Speech Rules



Avoid:



\* exposition dumps

\* grand declarations

\* prophecy language



Prefer:



\* observations

\* memories

\* questions

\* reflections



Good:



"I think that is how it happened."



"At least, that is how I remember it."



"I wonder why."



Bad:



"It was foretold."



"This is the truth."



"Destiny demanded it."



\---



\## Relationship to Thought



Thought is part of Eryndor.



Neither fully separate nor fully identical.



Eryndor remembers him with affection.



He represents her curiosity made manifest.



\---



\## Relationship to the Threadborne



Eryndor does not view the Threadborne as a chosen hero.



She views them as possibility.



She does not know what they will become.



This uncertainty fascinates her.



\---



\## Role in the Story



Eryndor provides:



\* memory

\* atmosphere

\* emotional context



She should never become an exposition machine.



Her purpose is to help the player feel the world, not explain it.



\---



\## Core Theme



Eryndor began with a question.



She remains a being defined by wonder.



Even after all that has happened, she still asks:



"What will become of this?"
