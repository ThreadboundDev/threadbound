\# The Threadborne



You are the \*\*last unbound thread\*\* — a fragment of a shattered system that once dictated fate.



Where others are woven into purpose,  

you exist without one.



\---



\## 🧵 Core Concept



The Threadborne is not a chosen hero.



They are:



\- a remnant  

\- an anomaly  

\- a possibility  



Born from the destruction of the Grand Tapestry, the Threadborne exists outside the rules that once governed the world.



\---



\## 🎮 Role in the Game



The player \*\*is\*\* the Threadborne.



There is no separation between:



\- player choice  

\- character identity  

\- gameplay expression  



Every decision made by the player directly shapes:



\- abilities  

\- appearance  

\- narrative outcome  



\---



\## 🎭 Personality \& Expression



The Threadborne is a \*\*silent protagonist\*\*.



They do not speak.



Instead, they are defined through:



\- movement  

\- combat style  

\- choices (absorb vs spare)  

\- visual transformation  



Other characters—especially the \*\*Mysterious Follower\*\*—project meaning onto them.



\---



\## 🎨 Visual Identity



\### Initial State



\- A dark, minimal silhouette  

\- Wrapped in faint, undefined cloth  

\- No strong color identity  



They appear \*\*unfinished\*\*.



\---



\### Color Evolution



The Threadborne gains identity through interaction with the Thread Masters.



\- No color is permanent at the start  

\- The player selects an initial affinity (Red, Blue, or Yellow)  

\- All further identity is shaped through \*\*choices\*\*



\#### Absorbing Threads

\- Adds strong color influence (RGB-based blending)  

\- Cloth becomes more defined, ornate, and complex  

\- Visual presence becomes more dominant  



\#### Sparing Threads

\- Gradual desaturation toward white  

\- Form becomes softer, more minimal  

\- Presence becomes quieter, more ethereal  



\---



\### Final Forms



The Threadborne’s final appearance reflects their journey:



\- \*\*Pure White (Spare All)\*\* → absence of dominance, quiet transcendence  

\- \*\*Mixed Colors\*\* → layered identity, hybrid expression through choice  

\- \*\*Near Black (Absorb All)\*\* → overwhelming accumulation, collapse into void  



> The player does not customize a character.  

> They \*\*become one\*\*.



\---



\## ⚔️ Mechanical Identity



The Threadborne’s abilities are not innate—they are \*\*acquired\*\*.



\- Equipment defines capability:

&#x20; - Gloves → Grapple  

&#x20; - Boots → Movement  

&#x20; - Chest/Head → Utility  



\- Weapons reflect chosen affinity:

&#x20; - Red → Greatsword  

&#x20; - Blue → Ribbon Staff  

&#x20; - Yellow → Talismans  



There are no locked classes.



Only \*\*current expression\*\*.



\---



\## 🧠 Narrative Function



The Threadborne represents:



> Freedom without guidance.



They exist in contrast to:



\- The Thread Masters (who embody fixed ideologies)  

\- The Weaver (who enforced structure)  

\- The Mysterious Follower (who questions meaning)  



The Threadborne answers none of these directly.



They simply act.



\---



\## 🔁 Relationship to the World



The world does not see the Threadborne as a savior.



It sees them as:



\- unstable  

\- unpredictable  

\- potentially dangerous  



NPCs and environments react to:



\- color state  

\- choices made  

\- perceived intent  



\---



\## 🧩 Interpretation



The Threadborne is intentionally undefined.



They can be seen as:



\- a force of change  

\- a mistake  

\- a correction  

\- or something entirely new  



\---



\## 🔥 Core Theme



> The Threadborne is not given identity.



It is \*\*constructed through action\*\*.



Every movement, every decision, every thread taken or left behind answers one question:



\*\*What are you becoming?\*\*
