# Narrative Voice & Lore Delivery – Threadbound

## Eryndor as Narrator

Eryndor serves as the primary narrative voice.

She is:

- memory
- witness
- world-consciousness

Her voice may appear:

- at save points
- meditation sites
- key locations
- moments of reflection

Delivery should feel:

- quiet
- reflective
- intimate
- poetic

Not instructional.

Not verbose.

The world speaks.

---

## Meditation / Reflection Sites

Inspired by reflective spaces.

Potential names:

- Memory Knots
- Woven Shrines
- Meditation Looms
- Resting Threads

At these spaces:

- camera may zoom
- audio quiets
- Eryndor speaks

Lore delivery should occur sparingly.

The goal is:

reflection rather than exposition dumping.

---

## Division of Narrative Roles

### Eryndor

Speaks of:

- memory
- place
- history
- emotional truth

She contextualizes the world.

---

### The Follower / Thought

Speaks of:

- identity
- philosophy
- freedom
- becoming

He gives:

- cryptic guidance
- questions
- personal reflection

He should never become an exposition machine.

The player should feel:

"He knows something I do not."

---

## Hidden Truth

The player may know:

Thought existed.

The player may not know:

Thought is the Follower.

This truth remains hidden until secret conditions are met.

The Follower is not merely mysterious.

He is:

The First Weaver.
