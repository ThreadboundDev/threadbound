# Lore \& World of Eryndor

Long ago, the world was bound by the **Grand Tapestry** — a living weave maintained by the Weaver at the Grand Loom. Three primordial threads dictated destiny:

* **Red Thread** of Power
* **Blue Thread** of Balance (sometimes called Fate)
* **Yellow Thread** of Essence (life force, memory, spirit)

In rebellion against predestination, the Weaver shattered the Loom. Magic faded, society fractured, and most lost their thread connection.

From the wreckage fell one final unbound thread — incarnated as **you**, the **Threadborne**.

Now, three **Thread Masters** rule the remnants. Strange **Reclaimers** (silkworm-like) devour loose threads to "repair" reality.

Your journey decides: restore fate, destroy it, or rewrite it forever.
