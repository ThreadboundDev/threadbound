# Curated assets for Threadbound — October 28, 2026

Chase prefers stylized/low-poly 3D and wants recommendations he can choose from,
rather than sorting the [Reddit directory](https://www.reddit.com/r/gamedev/comments/1m76pm4/the_ultimate_free_game_dev_asset_list_50_sites/)
himself. This report filters that directory by current needs, checks the primary
pages for the recommended releases, and separates useful additions from overlap.

**Recommendation: start with KayKit Character Animations, RPG Tools Bits and
Forest Nature Free.** They fill work motions, hand props and vegetation. Add
Kenney Particle Pack for effects experiments. Use the architecture, clothing,
weapons and NOX recordings already retained before collecting replacements.

License checks below are **listing-level**, checked October 28, 2026. These new
packs have not been downloaded, inspected, heard or imported in this pass.
Published counts are not independently measured counts. Preserve the actual
release's CC0 notice when collecting it; an unexplained discrepancy goes on hold.
Existing archive-level findings remain in the [library plan](cc0_library_and_search_plan.md).
All suggested uses and suitability rankings are our assessment, not creator claims.

## The remaining gaps worth investigating

The gap is now specificity, not a shortage of generic fantasy art. A future
focused pass should look for a **complete loom with shuttle/spindle**, **net or
rope repair props**, and clean **cloth tear, fabric stretch, rope tension and
spool rattle** recordings with explicit CC0 evidence. No such exact release was
cleared here. We can combine general work motions with authored hand contacts,
but should not label generic hammering as a ready-made weaving animation.

Choose a coherent visual recipe: restrained palette, broad readable forms,
consistent texel scale, simple shadows, and a few woven details. Separate that
surface treatment from polygon count. An imported model becomes Threadbound art
through selection and adaptation, not by naming a stock creature differently.
