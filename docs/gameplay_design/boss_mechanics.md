\# Boss Mechanics \& RTA Moments



Large bosses (Monarch, Hermit, Sage, Reclaimer, Weaver, Secret Follower superboss) feature cinematic RTA bursts (Based on available equip slots).



\- Trigger: Boss telegraph window (3–5 s).

\- Brief time pause (0.5–2 s, shorter on harder difficulty).

\- Queue 1–3 inputs or quick gear swap for a flashy finisher

\- Become available based on 'learned' equip slots + boss damage done



Examples: Big Grapple Slam, Pole Vault Strike, Omni-Slash, hot-swap into hybrid move.  

Hard Weaver fight becomes full arsenal showcase with rapid grapples, portals, and vaults.



Cinematics use painterly thread explosions and fraying health-bar visuals.
