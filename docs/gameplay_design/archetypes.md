\## Red – Power / Destruction (Monarch)

\- Charge Dash/Jump (Slow down then get burst movement)

\- Momentum Grapple (swing heavy chain to build 'momentum' tossing it pulls you in any direction regardless of hitting collider)

\- Unravel (tear red-thread walls/floors)





\## Blue – Balance / Connection (Hermit)

\- Pole Vault (Sun Wukong-style extendable silk staff)

  - Grounded: Vault or perch if not moving (stick to ceiling/wall, release any direction), if moving then pole-vault (Jump with extra initial height)

  - Midair: Downward reach → super pogo on ANY collider (longer range than universal pogo)

\- Silk Drift (slow-fall steering)

\- Silk Trampoline (connect two points to form a silk trampoline that you can bounce on)

\- Pendulum Grapple (Connect to any collider and pendulum swing, build momentum but swinging back and forth)









\## Yellow – Essence / Illusion (Sage)

\- Portal Leap (E33 grab + Portal Gun)

  - Raycast grab → toss with momentum → enter portal → directional exit (360° input)

\- Manifest temporary foothold / platform (any direction)  (Double jump)

\- Manifest temporary platform
