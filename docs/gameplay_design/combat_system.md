\# Combat System – Threadbound



Threadbound combat is designed to feel like \*\*Hollow Knight’s fluid, momentum-driven flow\*\* — never slowing the player down like traditional Metroid or Castlevania. Attacks chain naturally out of movement, thread momentum carries into strikes, and the rhythm stays fast and expressive. The goal is pure action: you’re always moving, always weaving, never stopping to “think” your way through a fight.



\## Core Philosophy

\- Combat is an extension of traversal, not a separate mode.

\- Every archetype feels distinct but the \*\*flow is universal\*\* — no slow methodical pacing.

\- Starting weapon (chosen in Chamber of the First Weave) only changes attack speed, range, and combo style.

\- Equipment slots (Gloves/Boots/Chest-Head) add flair or utility but never gate basic combat.

\- Boss fights add Expedition 33-style RTA cinematic bursts (brief pause + input queue) for spectacle without breaking flow.



\## Universal Combat Loop (All Archetypes)

1\. \*\*Basic Attack\*\* – Light attack (fast, combo-able).

2\. \*\*Heavy Attack\*\* – Stronger, slower wind-up but higher momentum (can be canceled into movement).

3\. \*\*Pogo\*\* – Universal downward strike on enemies (short range) — doubles as traversal.

4\. \*\*Thread Interaction\*\* – Grapple, vault, or portal can cancel into or out of attacks for seamless combos.

5\. \*\*Dodge / Thread Dash\*\* – Short invuln dash (tied to momentum) — no roll, just fluid repositioning.



Visuals: Painterly thread trails explode on every hit. Frayed cloth flutters with each swing. Hits feel weighty but snappy.



\## Starting Weapon Flavors (Only Affects Speed/Range/Combos)

| Starting Choice | Weapon          | Attack Speed | Range       | Combo Flavor                              | Visual/Feel                     |

|-----------------|-----------------|--------------|-------------|-------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|

| \*\*Red\*\*         | Sword / Scythe  | Fast         | Medium      | Wide slashes, momentum-charged spins      | Fiery red thread snaps, heavy impact |

| \*\*Blue\*\*        | Ribbon Staff    | Medium       | Long        | Whipping chains, pole-vault strikes       | Flowing blue silk arcs, graceful loops |

| \*\*Yellow\*\*      | Energy Fists    | Very Fast    | Short       | Rapid punches, portal-assisted blinks     | Golden rift punches, illusionary afterimages |



\- All weapons can be used in air/ground.

\- Combos build “thread tension” meter (visual glowing threads wrapping the player) — full meter unlocks a powerful finisher that ties into your current equipment.

\- No weapon is ever “locked out” — you can always fight effectively with any build.



\## Equipment \& Hybrid Flavor

\- Gloves (Grapple slot) can turn grapples into combat tools (Red pull-in slam, Blue pendulum whip, Yellow portal strike).

\- Boots (Jump slot) add aerial options (Red charge dive, Blue pole-vault strike, Yellow portal leap punch).

\- Chest/Head (Special slot) adds utility finishers (Red unravel ground-pound, Blue trampoline bounce attack, Yellow temp-platform trap).



Hybrids create unique signatures (Red+Yellow explosive portal fists, Blue+Yellow illusionary staff clones, etc.).



\## Boss Combat \& RTA Moments

Large bosses (Monarch, Hermit, Sage, Reclaimer, Weaver, Secret Follower) keep the same fast flow but add cinematic RTA bursts:

\- Telegraph window (3–5 s) → brief time pause (scales with difficulty).

\- Queue inputs or quick gear swap for flashy finishers (KH2 Final Form energy).

\- Hard Weaver = full arsenal showcase (rapid hot-swaps, grapples, portals, vaults).



\## Audio \& Visual Feedback

\- Every hit has satisfying thread “snap” + painterly glow burst.

\- Enemy stagger shows fraying threads.

\- Player silhouette evolves with palette (crimson fury, blue grace, golden mirages).



This keeps combat feeling like a natural extension of the joyful traversal loop — you’re always threading, always moving, never waiting.
