In particular, an attractive screenshot alone was not treated as proof of a final playable environment. Selection focused on Threadbound gameplay, its own art tests and its Blender work.

The 172-image screenshot review starts July 23 and runs through September 26. Earlier screenshots exist but were not all reviewed, and only the two videos above were sampled. Review sheets and their full source index remain under `Media/Development/devlog4/review/` for further selection.
