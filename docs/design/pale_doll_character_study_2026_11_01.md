## Chase's selected direction

November 1, 2026. Chase prefers the first pale lacquered articulated mannequin
concept over the warmer exposed-wood variant. Keep the visible wear and tear,
jointed hands, substantial carved limb volumes and oval two-eye doll head.
Both the raised hood and exposed head with fuller scarf collar are liked;
neither is selected as a permanent restriction on the protagonist.

Chase corrected the first sheet/body silhouette: this is a **boy doll, or at
least subtly masculine**. Use a flatter broader chest, straighter substantial
waist, compact narrower hips and less rounded upper thighs. Keep the agile
build, worn lacquer and crafted joints. V2 reference/model supersede the first
shape; this does not call for muscular anatomy or a change to the head design.
Chase subsequently likes the corrected direction and requests a clean single
front T-pose for 3D modeling. The [saved front reference](../../work/pale-doll-study-2026-11-01/Threadborne_Pale_Doll_TPose_Front_v2.png)
shows the unclothed doll on white, without equipment or inset views. This
records acceptance of the visual direction, not completed production-model fit.
Chase then requests the same T-pose with no eye holes. The
[blank-head reference](../../work/pale-doll-study-2026-11-01/Threadborne_Pale_Doll_TPose_Front_BlankHead_v3.png)
is the current input for modeling/generation. Keep the oval head, center seam
and worn lacquer; eye openings can be explored on masks separately. The existing
v2 3D study predates this face decision and still has its earlier eye sockets.

The protagonist's recognizable body can persist beneath different equipment.
Small worn clothing openings at elbows and knees may reveal doll construction;
outfits need not expose every joint or share identical damage. Masks or entire
head variants and Pattern embroidery remain explorations, not selected slots
or new mechanics. Preserve the existing Identity/Equipment/Pattern separation.

Chase describes the opening as the cut thread falling onto a crafted doll and
fusing with it to become the Threadborne. Clothing weaves onto the awakening
body, followed by orientation/movement and finding the first weapons. This
records his intended opening image. The maker, reason for abandonment, and
lone-body versus discarded-body-pile setting remain undecided. The cinematic,
canonical story files and tutorial implementation have not been rewritten.

## Authorized study and result

Chase approved one consistent modeling sheet followed by a simple separate
3D body test using the existing skeleton and animations. The
[study package](../../work/pale-doll-study-2026-11-01/README.md) retains the chosen
concepts and provides the modeling sheet, measured source inventory, editable
Blender body, separate export and genuine rest/pose/motion renders.

The current export's clothed body is one large textured mesh. The study
therefore builds a separate articulated body around the unchanged 62-bone
skeleton rather than assuming removable clothes already reveal a complete
mannequin. All 48 source clips remain. Twenty-one build checks and independent
GLB validation (18 additional checks) are recorded in the package. Generated reference views guide
appearance; the measured rig and actual 3D views govern dimensions.

This is a body blockout and rig compatibility sample. It is not a finished
sculpt, fitted modular outfit, revised animation delivery or installed player.
Shoulder/hip/torso clearance, weapon grip fit and the live posture corrections
still need review before a production replacement. The procedural lacquer
finish is native Blender material work; the GLB carries flat colors until a
texture-authoring/baking pass is scoped.
