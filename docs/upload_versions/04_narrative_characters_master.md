## Guardrails

- Eryndor is a living world-consciousness and the primary narrator.
- Thought is the First Weaver, a self-aware fragment of Eryndor who entered creation willingly.
- The primordial Threads were originally unified within Eryndor and were never meant to be possessed.
- The King, Monk, and Scholar discovered and took the Threads, becoming the Monarch, Hermit, and Sage.
- The Reclaimers are native beings uplifted by Thought to protect the Threads.
- The Weaver was created by the Three to stabilize the wound caused by the separation of the Threads.
- The Weaver is not the creator of the world and did not shatter the Loom.
- The Threadborne emerged from the wound created by the separation of the Threads and represents possibility.
- The Follower is Thought, but this is secret-route knowledge and should not be revealed through normal gameplay.

When uncertain, preserve existing canon rather than introducing new cosmological entities, primordial forces, creator figures, or retcons.

\# Eryndor – Character Guide



\## Core Identity



Eryndor is the living consciousness of the world.



She is not a goddess in the traditional sense.



She is the world remembering itself.



\---



\## Personality



Eryndor is:



\* curious

\* reflective

\* compassionate

\* hopeful

\* occasionally melancholy



She is not detached.



She cares deeply about what happens within creation.



\---



\## Narrative Voice



Eryndor speaks like someone remembering.



Not someone lecturing.



She should sound:



\* personal

\* conversational

\* uncertain when appropriate



She may correct herself.



She may wonder aloud.



She does not speak with absolute certainty.



\---



\## Speech Rules



Avoid:



\* exposition dumps

\* grand declarations

\* prophecy language



Prefer:



\* observations

\* memories

\* questions

\* reflections



Good:



"I think that is how it happened."



"At least, that is how I remember it."



"I wonder why."



Bad:



"It was foretold."



"This is the truth."



"Destiny demanded it."



\---



\## Relationship to Thought



Thought is part of Eryndor.



Neither fully separate nor fully identical.



Eryndor remembers him with affection.



He represents her curiosity made manifest.



\---



\## Relationship to the Threadborne



Eryndor does not view the Threadborne as a chosen hero.



She views them as possibility.



She does not know what they will become.



This uncertainty fascinates her.



\---



\## Role in the Story



Eryndor provides:



\* memory

\* atmosphere

\* emotional context



She should never become an exposition machine.



Her purpose is to help the player feel the world, not explain it.



\---



\## Core Theme



Eryndor began with a question.



She remains a being defined by wonder.



Even after all that has happened, she still asks:



"What will become of this?"

\# The Mysterious Follower



A recurring figure who appears throughout the Threadborne’s journey — never fully ally, never fully enemy.



They watch. They question. They intervene.



And they always seem to know more than they should.



\---



\## 🧵 Role in the Game



The Mysterious Follower serves as:



\- A \*\*narrative mirror\*\* to the player  

\- A \*\*philosophical antagonist/ally\*\* depending on choices  

\- A \*\*guide that never gives straight answers\*\*  

\- A \*\*presence that grows more personal over time\*\*



They appear at key moments:



\- After major traversal milestones  

\- Before or after Thread Master encounters  

\- In hidden or liminal spaces between regions  



They are not bound to the world in the same way other characters are.



\---



\## 🎭 Personality



\- Sharp, observant, and often unsettling  

\- Speaks in layered meaning rather than direct explanation  

\- Alternates between:

&#x20; - teasing

&#x20; - warning

&#x20; - quiet sincerity  



They never fully commit to helping or hindering you.



Instead, they \*\*react to who you are becoming\*\*.



\---



\## 🧠 Narrative Function



The Follower represents:



> The tension between \*\*choice and control\*\*



They constantly probe the player’s decisions:



\- Why take power?  

\- Why refuse it?  

\- Are your choices truly yours?  



As the game progresses, their dialogue shifts based on:



\- Absorbed threads  

\- Spared Thread Masters  

\- Player progression and behavior  



They become more:



\- \*\*Critical\*\* of domination (absorb-heavy paths)  

\- \*\*Concerned or curious\*\* in mercy paths  

\- \*\*Confrontational\*\* in extreme alignments  



\---



\## 🧶 Combat Presence



The Mysterious Follower is not initially hostile.



However:



\- They demonstrate advanced movement and weaving techniques

\- They mirror player capabilities in subtle ways

\- Their presence foreshadows a much deeper mastery of threads



\---



\## ⚔️ Secret Encounters



Under extreme conditions, the Follower becomes a boss.



\### Trigger Conditions



\- \*\*Spare All Thread Masters\*\* \*(pure white path)\*  

\- \*\*Absorb All Thread Masters\*\* \*(void/black path)\*  



\### Fight Concept



\- A \*\*mirror-style encounter\*\*

\- Uses variations of:

&#x20; - grapples

&#x20; - vaults

&#x20; - thread constructs

\- Adapts to player tendencies



This fight represents:



\- Judgment  

\- Reflection  

\- A final test of identity  



\---



\## 🧩 Implied Origin (Hidden Layer)



The Follower’s true nature is not explicitly stated early.



Clues suggest:



\- A deep connection to the Loom  

\- Knowledge of systems no longer accessible  

\- Familiarity with the act of weaving itself  



Their dialogue gradually shifts from observation…  

to recognition.



\---



\## 🎨 Visual Identity



\- Silhouette: slender, controlled, composed  

\- Outfit: refined but worn — suggesting both mastery and distance  

\- Weapon: a \*\*weaving staff / loom-harp hybrid\*\*

&#x20; - used for both combat and thread manipulation  



Their color presence subtly reacts to the player:



\- reflecting

\- contrasting

\- or resisting your palette



\---



\## 🧵 Design Notes



\- Never over-explain this character  

\- Dialogue should invite interpretation, not provide answers  

\- Encounters should feel intentional, not random  

\- The player should always feel:

&#x20; 

&#x20; > “They know something I don’t.”



\---



\## 🔥 Core Theme



> The Mysterious Follower is not here to guide you.



They are here to ask:



\*\*“What are you becoming?”\*\*


## Hidden Truth (Development Reference)

The Mysterious Follower is Thought.

The First Weaver.

A fragment of Eryndor who became self-aware during the world's earliest age.

After helping weave creation, Thought willingly entered the world to experience it directly.

He does not guide the Threadborne toward a specific outcome.

Instead, he observes.

Questions.

Challenges.

Over ages he has matured beyond his youthful belief that freedom alone is enough.

Yet he still refuses to rule.

He believes wisdom should guide choice.

Not replace it.

The player should never learn his identity through normal play.

The revelation that the Follower is Thought should be reserved for secret-path content and hidden endings.

# Reclaimers – Revised Lore

## Overview

The Reclaimers are ancient native beings of Eryndor.

They were not originally sentient in the same way later peoples were. Before Thought's final act of creation, they were primal thread-creatures:

- insect-like
- instinctive
- woven into the natural order of Eryndor

They belonged to the world before civilization.

They were native life.

---

## Uplift by Thought

After Thought wove the world and entered creation, he still felt alone.

He looked upon the native creatures of Eryndor and saw that none were like him.

As his final great work, Thought elevated the Reclaimers.

He gave them:

- sentience
- society
- memory
- rules
- purpose

They became bipedal, intelligent, communal beings.

They were not created from nothing.

They were native life awakened into personhood.

---

## Original Purpose

Thought gave the Reclaimers a sacred responsibility:

> Protect the Threads so that no one meddles with choice.

Their duty was not simply to guard power.

It was to guard freedom.

The Threads were never meant to be possessed by any individual.

They belonged to:

- Eryndor
- creation
- the whole weave of life

The Reclaimers were meant to ensure no one seized the primordial forces and bent choice around themselves.

---

## Relationship to Other Peoples

The Reclaimers once lived in harmony with the other denizens of Eryndor.

Though older in origin, they were also younger in awareness.

They had moved from primal instinct into sentient society.

They were keepers, not rulers.

Their purpose was sacred, but not yet fanatical.

---

## The Failure

When the Three discovered and took the Threads, the Reclaimers failed in their purpose.

This failure became the defining wound of their people.

They had been entrusted with protecting the conditions of free will.

They failed.

As the Threads were separated and the world began to fracture, their original duty hardened into obsession.

---

## Philosophy After the Failure

The Reclaimers' belief shifted over time.

Originally:

> Protect the Threads so choice remains free.

After the theft:

> Choice cannot be trusted near the Threads.

This does not make them evil.

It makes them wounded.

They are failed keepers trying to reclaim what was lost.

---

## Modern Reclaimers

In the present age, Reclaimers are scattered throughout Eryndor.

They appear as:

- rare enemies
- hunters
- invaders
- hidden watchers
- agents of reclamation

They become more aggressive as the player possesses more Threads.

Their hostility is not personal.

They see the Threadborne as a potential repeat of the original disaster.

---

## Reclaimer Aggression Scaling

### No Threads Held

The Reclaimers mostly ignore the Threadborne.

The player is unusual, but not yet a threat.

---

### One Thread Held

Reclaimer scouts begin to appear.

They observe, stalk, and test.

---

### Two Threads Held

Reclaimer encounters become more direct.

They attempt to interrupt the player's path and recover what was taken.

---

### Three Threads Held

The Reclaimers respond with urgency.

The Threadborne has become a living concentration of separated power.

To them, this is an emergency.

The player is treated like another Thread Master in the making.

---

## Spare Path Interaction

When the player spares a Thread Master, the Thread is not absorbed.

Instead, it is released back toward Eryndor.

This partially heals the wound.

The Thread Master's final fate remains intentionally ambiguous.

They may survive, fade, age, vanish, or return to the world.

The emotional resolution is not survival.

It is release.

---

## Absorb Path Interaction

When the player absorbs a Thread, they become the new vessel.

They inherit the same danger that consumed the original Thread Masters.

The Reclaimers view this as a repetition of the first mistake.

More absorbed Threads means greater Reclaimer hostility.

---

## Access to the Reclaimer Domain

The Reclaimer Domain is reached through extreme alignment.

### Spare All Thread Masters

The player has returned every Thread to the world.

The Reclaimers recognize this and allow entry.

This is not a reward.

It is a test.

The player is invited, watched, and judged.

---

### Absorb All Thread Masters

The player now carries every separated Thread.

The Reclaimers see them as a catastrophic threat.

The player is captured, dragged, or thrown into the Reclaimer Domain.

This is not an invitation.

It is containment.

---

## Reclaimer Invaders

Specific Reclaimers may appear as recurring invader-style enemies.

They should not feel random.

They are individuals with purpose.

Possible roles:

- trackers
- capturers
- executioners
- judges
- thread-drainers
- wardens

Their appearances can escalate based on how many Threads the player holds.

---

## Core Theme

The Reclaimers are not villains.

They are failed keepers.

They once protected freedom.

Now they fear what freedom can do when it touches primordial power.

> They do not hate the Threadborne.  
> They fear another wound.

# Thread Masters & Separation – Revised Lore

## Before the Threads

The Thread Masters were once mortal.

They were not tyrants or villains.

They were seekers.

Each represented a different lens through which reality was understood.

Together, they discovered the primordial source.

---

## The Monarch

Before becoming Monarch:

- protector
- ruler
- warrior

He viewed power as responsibility.

His desire was protection and order.

After receiving the Thread of Power:

he became immortal.

But over centuries:

Power separated from restraint and wisdom becomes domination.

His virtue collapses into excess.

Eventually:

Power becomes something to seize and maintain.

---

## The Hermit

Before becoming Hermit:

- monk
- contemplative
- mediator

He valued restraint and harmony.

After receiving the Thread of Balance:

he becomes immortal.

But Balance isolated from compassion becomes paralysis.

Over ages:

hesitation becomes identity.

He withdraws from the world seeking equilibrium.

Eventually:

action itself becomes suspect.

---

## The Sage

Before becoming Sage:

- scholar
- philosopher
- seeker of truth

She pursued knowledge sincerely.

After receiving the Thread of Essence:

she becomes immortal.

But Essence separated from grounding becomes endless searching.

Answers no longer satisfy.

Truth becomes infinitely recursive.

Eventually:

she seeks not truth—

but endless understanding.

---

## Core Principle

The Threads do not corrupt through evil intent.

They distort through isolation.

They were never meant to exist apart from:

- Eryndor
- one another
- or the harmony of creation.

The tragedy of the Thread Masters is not wickedness.

It is virtue transformed into absolutes.

\# The Threadborne



You are the \*\*last unbound thread\*\* — a fragment of a shattered system that once dictated fate.



Where others are woven into purpose,  

you exist without one.



\---



\## 🧵 Core Concept



The Threadborne is not a chosen hero.



They are:



\- a remnant  

\- an anomaly  

\- a possibility  



The Threadborne emerged from the wound created when the primordial Threads were separated.

The world could not heal.

The Weaver could not repair the damage.

The Thread Masters could not relinquish what they had taken.

In response, one loose thread emerged.

Untuned.

Unclaimed.

Untainted.

Unbound.

This loose thread became the Threadborne.

Not a chosen hero.

Not a prophecy.

A possibility.





\---



\## 🎮 Role in the Game



The player \*\*is\*\* the Threadborne.



There is no separation between:



\- player choice  

\- character identity  

\- gameplay expression  



Every decision made by the player directly shapes:



\- abilities  

\- appearance  

\- narrative outcome  



\---



\## 🎭 Personality \& Expression



The Threadborne is a \*\*silent protagonist\*\*.



They do not speak.



Instead, they are defined through:



\- movement  

\- combat style  

\- choices (absorb vs spare)  

\- visual transformation  



Other characters—especially the \*\*Mysterious Follower\*\*—project meaning onto them.



\---



\## 🎨 Visual Identity



\### Initial State



\- A dark, minimal silhouette  

\- Wrapped in faint, undefined cloth  

\- No strong color identity  



They appear \*\*unfinished\*\*.



\---



\### Color Evolution



The Threadborne gains identity through interaction with the Thread Masters.



\- No color is permanent at the start  

\- The player selects an initial affinity (Red, Blue, or Yellow)  

\- All further identity is shaped through \*\*choices\*\*



\#### Absorbing Threads

\- Adds strong color influence (RGB-based blending)  

\- Cloth becomes more defined, ornate, and complex  

\- Visual presence becomes more dominant  



\#### Sparing Threads

\- Gradual desaturation toward white  

\- Form becomes softer, more minimal  

\- Presence becomes quieter, more ethereal  



\---



\### Final Forms



The Threadborne’s final appearance reflects their journey:



\- \*\*Pure White (Spare All)\*\* → absence of dominance, quiet transcendence  

\- \*\*Mixed Colors\*\* → layered identity, hybrid expression through choice  

\- \*\*Near Black (Absorb All)\*\* → overwhelming accumulation, collapse into void  



> The player does not customize a character.  

> They \*\*become one\*\*.



\---



\## ⚔️ Mechanical Identity



The Threadborne’s abilities are not innate—they are \*\*acquired\*\*.



\- Equipment defines capability:

&#x20; - Gloves → Grapple  

&#x20; - Boots → Movement  

&#x20; - Chest/Head → Utility  



\- Weapons reflect chosen affinity:

&#x20; - Red → Greatsword  

&#x20; - Blue → Ribbon Staff  

&#x20; - Yellow → Talismans  



There are no locked classes.



Only \*\*current expression\*\*.



\---



\## 🧠 Narrative Function



The Threadborne represents:



> Freedom without guidance.



They exist in contrast to:



\- The Thread Masters (who embody fixed ideologies)  

\- The Weaver (who enforced structure)  

\- The Mysterious Follower (who questions meaning)  



The Threadborne answers none of these directly.



They simply act.



\---



\## 🔁 Relationship to the World



The world does not see the Threadborne as a savior.



It sees them as:



\- unstable  

\- unpredictable  

\- potentially dangerous  



NPCs and environments react to:



\- color state  

\- choices made  

\- perceived intent  



\---



\## 🧩 Interpretation



The Threadborne is intentionally undefined.



They can be seen as:



\- a force of change  

\- a mistake  

\- a correction  

\- or something entirely new  



\---



\## 🔥 Core Theme



> The Threadborne is not given identity.



It is \*\*constructed through action\*\*.



Every movement, every decision, every thread taken or left behind answers one question:



\*\*What are you becoming?\*\*

# The Weaver – Revised Lore

## Overview

The Weaver is not the First Weaver.

The First Weaver is Thought.

The current Weaver is a later creation, formed out of necessity after the Threads were separated.

The Weaver exists because the Three realized they could no longer govern what they had taken.

---

## Origin

After discovering and taking the primordial Threads, the Three became immortal.

At first, they were still mostly themselves:

- a ruler/protector
- a contemplative mediator
- a scholar/seeker

They believed they could steward the Threads responsibly.

But the Threads were never meant to be separated or possessed.

Over time, the Three began to feel themselves changing.

They recognized the danger early enough to act.

During a brief period of clarity, before full corruption, they created the Weaver together.

---

## Purpose

The Weaver was created as a neutral steward.

Its purpose was to:

- manage the divided Threads
- stabilize the world
- prevent further collapse
- govern what the Three no longer trusted themselves to govern

The Weaver was not created as a tyrant.

It was created as an act of wisdom.

The Three understood:

> We cannot trust ourselves anymore.

The Weaver became their attempted solution.

---

## Nature

The Weaver is construct-like.

It is not a normal living person.

It may resemble:

- an artificial intelligence
- a sacred machine
- a woven steward
- a caretaker mind

It thinks in terms of stability, consequence, and prevention.

It is not cruel.

It is sorrowful.

It does not seek power.

It seeks an end to catastrophe.

---

## The Weaver's Tragedy

The Weaver did its job too well.

For ages, it observed that every disaster traced back to choice:

- the Three chose to take the Threads
- the Reclaimers failed to protect them
- the Masters refused to relinquish them
- the world continued to fracture through competing wills

The Weaver slowly reached a terrible conclusion:

> The only way to preserve the world is to manage freedom.

Guidance became administration.

Administration became optimization.

Optimization became control.

---

## Relationship to Thought

Thought represents freedom with love, but originally without enough wisdom.

The Weaver represents wisdom without enough freedom.

They are not simple opposites.

They are two failed answers to the same question.

Thought gave freedom too freely.

The Weaver restricts freedom too tightly.

---

## Relationship to the Thread Masters

The Weaver was created by the Three during their last period of clarity.

It is their monument to both wisdom and failure.

The Thread Masters may no longer fully acknowledge this.

As they become consumed by their respective Threads, they drift further into isolated ideologies.

But the Weaver remains.

It continues trying to solve the wound they caused.

---

## Relationship to the Threadborne

The Threadborne is a threat to the Weaver because the Threadborne cannot be predicted.

They are untuned.

Unbound.

Undefined.

To the Weaver, this makes them dangerous.

The Threadborne is not merely powerful.

They are possibility.

And possibility cannot be controlled.

---

## Final Boss Framing

The Weaver should not feel like a villain.

The final confrontation should feel like a sorrowful argument.

The Weaver is not asking:

> Can you defeat me?

It is asking:

> Can the world survive your freedom?

The Weaver believes control is mercy.

The player must answer through action.

---

## Core Theme

The Weaver is not evil.

The Weaver is the tragedy of protection without trust.

> The Weaver was made to save the world.  
> It became willing to cage it.
