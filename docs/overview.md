# Threadbound – Overview

**A painterly 2D Metroidvania** built in Godot 4.5xx, with 2.5D parallax layers and Hollow Knight-inspired movement feel. Explore a fraying world of fate and redemption, where every thread you weave (or sever) reshapes your path, abilities, and ending.

## Core Themes & Aesthetics

### Themes
- **Fate vs Free Will** – The Tapestry metaphor: a world once fully woven by predestined threads, now unraveling after the Weaver's rebellion.
- **The Cost of Power** – Absorption corrupts; gaining strength from defeated Thread Masters comes with narrative and mechanical consequences.
- **Sacrifice vs Dominion** – Spare a Master to preserve connection, or absorb to claim dominance.
- **Community vs Isolation** – Choices echo through the world, affecting allies, environments, and multiple endings.

### Aesthetics
- **Painterly 2D** with muted, fraying backgrounds contrasted by **vibrant glowing threads**.
- Silk, cloth, and loom motifs everywhere — flowing animations, frayed edges, woven patterns.
- Stained-glass-style storytelling sequences for key memories and revelations.
- **Influences**: Hollow Knight (tight platforming & atmosphere), Gris (emotional color language), Ori (fluid movement & visuals), Demon Slayer (cloth/flow effects), Journey (minimalist emotional weight).

## Project Vision

You are the last free thread in a shattered loom. Choose your starting color and explore nonlinear regions ruled by the three Thread Masters:  
- **Monarch** (Red – Power)  
- **Hermit** (Blue – Balance)  
- **Sage** (Yellow – Essence)  

Decide for each: **Spare** (gain alliance, limited power) or **Absorb** (claim their thread, unlock hybrid abilities, risk corruption).  

Every choice reshapes:  
- Your traversal toolkit (charge jumps, silk drifts, manifest footholds, reverse grapples)  
- Narrative branches  
- Endings (8+ variations: True single-color, hybrid balances, black/white extremes, Reclaimers-zone unlocks)

**Tone**: Melancholic yet hopeful, painterly beauty meets subtle Christian undertones of creation, rebellion, and redemption — never heavy-handed.
