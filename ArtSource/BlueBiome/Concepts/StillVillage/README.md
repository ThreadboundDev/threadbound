## Review observations

- Keep backgrounds lighter and lower-contrast than the gameplay layer.
- The central concept perch appears unsupported; resolve it with a rear support or existing architecture during assembly.
- The four bulbs must retain their actual scene coordinates.
- The kit's wheel is a complete wheel despite its generated "wheel segment" label.
- The grass concept is broad-leaf undergrowth; model a lower grass variation if it crowds foot-level visibility.
- Bulb current is the moving visual accent; keep lake and dry machinery still.
