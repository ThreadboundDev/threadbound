# Woven Blue wood and rope bridge

The kit contains two broad wood texture fields, three tall supports,
and three bridge pieces. The warm amber and damp cedar fields work like
the cave textures when clipped inside a painted wall or floor. The
supports are a straight cedar post, cross-braced trestle, and forked pier.

At native scale, place a **Start** cap, then any number of **Repeat**
segments, then **End**. Each cap is 144 px wide and each repeat adds
744 px and two bays. All three images are 433 px tall. Align their top
edges on the same row and put each next layer at the previous layer's x
plus its full width. The standing line is 289 px below the shared top
edge. For an arbitrary scale, scale the assembled group together.

The three pieces come from one painted span. The repeat's edge columns
match pixel for pixel, and the deck is opaque across the tested standing
band. The layered example contains three repeats and two caps.

The temporary Godot trial uses the Blue shoreline backdrop and current
player. Normal movement reached the far cap across all three repeats.
A live frame confirmed the wood and bridge rendered in the game.
