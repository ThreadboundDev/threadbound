# Woven Blue terrain chunks

Each piece is one native-resolution ImageGen render with its clear margins
trimmed. The PNGs preserve the original artwork and alpha at their delivered
sizes. Import the PNGs as paint layers for the full pixel dimensions. The
optional [brush bundle](Threadbound_Woven_Blue_Terrain_Chunks.bundle) adds
the **Woven Blue - Terrain Chunks** tag for stamp placement in Krita.

| Chunk | Native PNG | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Chamber Exit Cliff](chunks/chamber_exit_cliff.png) | 1452 × 868 | Upper-left cave mouth, landing, and stepped shelf in one piece |
| [Stepped Connector Bank](chunks/stepped_connector_bank.png) | 2127 × 671 | Broad receiving apron, gentle uphill ramp, and higher shelf |
| [Deep Shore Headland](chunks/deep_shore_headland.png) | 1730 × 757 | Wide walkable rim with a continuous slate face tapering into the water |

[Open the native-scale join sample in Krita](Terrain_Join.ora) or
[preview the assembled route](connected-route.jpg). The connector's apron
overlaps behind the Chamber landing. Match the middle of the visible
standing planes and bury the connection in that overlap. The two connector
layers use opposite sides of the same native image, with their inner edges
meeting exactly. Keep them together when moving the connector as one piece.

The example verifies a continuous overlap at the landing and a pixel-exact
reconstruction of both connector sections. Check the standing planes
against actual player contact when placing them in the blockout. The
authored collision may need adjustments for these silhouettes.
