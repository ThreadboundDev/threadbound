# Blue one-pass paintover

[Krita source](Blue_One_Pass.kra) retains the authored Materials, Line Art, and Bridge Supports layers.
The guide layers are available in Krita. Godot uses the three art layers at their original scale and
uses the one-way guide to place the plank crossings. The exported ink retains its visible Krita
weight when Godot draws it in front of the player.

The house sits on the eastern cobble terrace. The cave lookout sits in the high west recess.
The entrance frames the cave spawn. All three props were made with built-in ImageGen using
the author’s painted layout as a style reference.
