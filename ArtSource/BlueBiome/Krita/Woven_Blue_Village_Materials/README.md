# Woven Blue Village Materials

Four material fills and four matching grassy or mossy tops for painting Blue in Krita. Each stamp is an individual full-color transparent brush with its own full-resolution PNG in [stamps](stamps/).

| Material | Where to use it | Matching top |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cave Slate | Cave walls, shaded ledges, exposed blue-violet rock | Moss |
| Village Cobble | Paved lanes, steps, low retaining walls | Grass |
| Honey Limestone | Warm terraces, cut stone, sunlit ledges | Moss |
| Packed Path | Dirt paths and gravel banks | Grass |

## Painting from the bottom up

1. Select the terrain interior from your outline and stamp on a separate fill layer. Start at the bottom and overlap the feathered edges. Shift the next stamp a little to keep repeated details from lining up.
2. On a layer above the fill, stamp the matching top only along the exposed upper edge. Start with the same brush width you used for the fill and overlap the top's stone or soil with the filled area.
3. Keep the top below your outline when you want a clean ink edge. If you want a few grass tips to cross the silhouette, clear the selection and place those on their own layer.

The [catalog](catalog.jpg) shows each pair and an example of the stamps overlapping. The [manifest](manifest.json) records the dimensions and prompts used with the built-in ImageGen tool.

## Krita brush presets

Open [the bundle](Threadbound_Woven_Blue_Village_Materials.bundle) through **Settings → Manage Resource Libraries → Import** on another Krita installation. In **Brush Presets**, choose **Woven Blue - Material Fills** or **Woven Blue - Grassy Material Tops** from the tag menu.
