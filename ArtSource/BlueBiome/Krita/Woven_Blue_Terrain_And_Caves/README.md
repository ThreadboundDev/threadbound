# Woven Blue terrain and caves

The expansion adds 31 full-color stamps to the Woven Blue library. It has
nine solid platforms, nine floating ledges, nine cave edges and four rear
cave textures. The rock pieces share blue-violet slate, warm apricot stone,
diagonal ochre weave and pockets of bright moss. The dark and damp rear
fields carry the same facets into the cave interior.

Import the bundle through **Settings → Manage Resource Libraries → Import**.
In **Brush Presets**, choose **Woven Blue - Solid Platforms**,
**Woven Blue - Floating Platforms**, **Woven Blue - Cave Edges**, or
**Woven Blue - Cave Textures**. The combined **Threadbound - Woven Blue** tag
also includes the [original 36-stamp set](../Woven_Blue_Stamps/README.md).
Single-click on a paint layer and adjust the toolbar Size to fit your blockout.

Start with two overlapping instances of **Rear Slate Field** at the same
scale. Add the shadow, copper vein, or damp fields where the wall changes
depth. Keep the front wall, ceiling and floor on separate layers. Use a
buttress, pillar or stalactite to cover a join and carry the same stone pattern
through it. For walkable surfaces, align the standing line inside the
visible upper plane so the boots have space on either side. The layered
example shows one way to stack the pieces. You can move any of its 16 layers,
duplicate a rear texture, or hide the preview matte.

The artwork was made with the built-in image generator using the revised
Woven Blue material and Chase's November 21 screenshots as references. Every
standalone cutout was checked for unclipped edges and every brush tip matches
the RGBA pixels in its PNG. The layered example was checked by recomposing
its independent layers.
