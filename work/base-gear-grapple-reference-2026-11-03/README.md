## Accepted authoring direction

Gloves/bracers include the attachment point. Each glove set owns its matching
grapple assembly and grapple behavior; swapping gloves swaps that assembly.
The shared mounting convention does not make every set's grapple identical or
reduce the differences to cosmetics. The existing equipment notes distinguish
Base standard, Red momentum-chain, Blue pendulum and Yellow snap grapples.

The coil, needle/hook and active extending line remain separable for animation.
The images propose placement and proportions, not a final mechanism or rig.
Fit the actual parts against the master skeleton, wrist/elbow movement and
shield clearance before cutting/rigging or replacing any runtime equipment.
No fixed physical dimensions or seam coordinates are established by these PNGs.

The approved color direction follows [Identity and appearance](../../docs/design/identity_and_appearance.md)
and the [Pattern design](../../docs/design/pattern_system.md): equipment supplies
shape and behavior; choice-driven Identity supplies the coordinated palette;
Patterns supply separate embroidery. Main cloth, secondary cloth and trim use
consistent color roles across sets. Wood, leather and metal retain their natural
appearance. Material slots or painted masks must be authored after 3D generation;
ImageGen/Tripo output is not assumed to contain reliable recoloring controls.
The four-color preview is a subset: later checks also need pale/spared, blended
and blackened/absorbed-all states from the existing Identity design.
