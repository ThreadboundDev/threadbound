# House + working villager art study

Open `renders/house_villager_side_by_side.png` for the overall comparison and
`renders/worker_motion.gif` for the four-second work cycle at a closer scale.
The left painted worker remains static. The right model is actually rigged and
animated. See `docs/art/cc0_house_villager_comparison_2026_10_28.md` in the repository
for sources, adaptation, limitations and reproduction commands.

`CC0_House_WorkingVillager_Comparison.blend` is the packed, editable source.
Open it in Blender and press Space to play frames 1–96. The rig action is
`Threadbound_Work_Plane_Loop_96f`. This directory is ignored by Godot through
`.gdignore`; no production assets have been replaced. The study is awaiting
Chase's appearance/motion review and does not approve a permanent 3D conversion.
