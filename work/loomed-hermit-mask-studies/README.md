# Loomed and Hermit mask studies

- [The Loomed and knotwork](loomed-mask-knotwork.png) uses warm ivory, a brow
  knot, and interlocking cream and gold stitching. Its proposed bonus scales
  with Base pieces.
- [Hermit and waves](hermit-mask-waves.png) keeps the oval and gold repair,
  adding a slate-indigo brushstroke and a flowing wave embroidery repeat.
  Its proposed bonus scales with Blue pieces.

Use these as visual references for one fitted Blender shell and two finishes.
Fit the rim and eye openings to the doll in both the Base cowl and Hermit hat.
Translate the embroidery into clean repeatable texture strips and check it on
the Base waist wrap and Hermit sash or sleeve. Start with full Base, full
Hermit, and a mixed set at gameplay zoom. The swatches are concept art for the
[Mask + Pattern test](../../docs/design/pattern_system.md).

## Image prompts

Both references were made with built-in ImageGen. Loomed used Chase's
ten-mask concept sheet as a reference. Hermit used the Loomed image as its
edit source to keep the shell consistent.

### Loomed

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one clean front-facing asset reference for Threadbound's Loomed mask and its paired textile embroidery. The attached ten-mask sheet is visual inspiration only, specifically its Loomed mask and interlocking knotwork. Use a warm gray matte backdrop. Show exactly two separated objects: a large isolated straight-on orthographic porcelain mask in the upper 75% of the image and one clearly separate horizontal cloth strip below it. Center the mask. Compact elongated oval doll face with rounded forehead, softened tapered chin, two narrow dark almond eye openings. Low-profile shallow fitted shell, no nose projection, horns, helmet, head, straps, or decorations outside its oval. Antique warm ivory porcelain with restrained satin glaze. Show two subtly different ivory shards meeting at one clear asymmetrical repaired seam of warm gold, running from the upper-right forehead down diagonally toward the lower-left cheek with one short fine branch. Keep the mask mostly ivory. A small understated interlocking knot painted in warm umber at the brow echoes the strip. The textile strip is a flat straight-on sample of three big repeating interlocking knot motifs in dull warm gold and cream thread stitched on earthy charcoal-brown woven cloth. Restrained gold and convincing fine stitches. The motif must read at small scale. Clear grounded crafted fantasy. Diffuse neutral light, no exaggerated shadows, no perspective angle, no text, no labels, no sheet border, no other objects.

### Hermit

Use case: precise-object-edit. Create the matching Threadbound Hermit mask and wave embroidery reference. The input image is the exact shell and layout master. Preserve its framing, front orthographic camera, oval outer silhouette including width and chin, exact eye openings and their positions, porcelain thickness, the location and branching of the irregular diagonal gold repair, and warm gray background. Change the decorative finish and textile strip only. Remove the symmetry and central brow knot and central straight decoration. On the satin warm ivory porcelain, paint one bold slate-indigo wave that curls once from the upper left and sweeps down along the left cheek, with a shorter balanced curve at the lower right. Retain ample clean ivory, preserve the dark eye openings and allow the existing asymmetric gold repair to cross both ivory and blue. Treat the wave as brushwork on the glaze, using dark muted indigo to match a fantasy monk's indigo cloth. Keep the existing asymmetrical repaired seam as physical gold distinct from the painted blue. Replace the horizontal knotwork strip with a matching flat cloth embroidery sample of three clearly repeating simple paired flowing waves in muted blue and warm ivory thread on deep slate-indigo woven cloth, understated edge stitches. Preserve the separate placement of mask and cloth strip. Two objects only, no lettering, labels, border, straps or props.
