# Loomed and Hermit mask studies

- [The Loomed and knotwork](loomed-mask-knotwork.png) uses warm ivory, a brow
  knot, a long diagonal gold repair, and interlocking cream and gold stitching.
  Its proposed bonus scales with Base pieces.
- [Hermit and waves](hermit-mask-waves-v2.png) shares the oval shell and has
  a gold repair joining the lower right cheek to the chin. The slate-indigo
  brushstroke and wave embroidery carry the motif. Its proposed bonus scales
  with Blue pieces. The [first study](hermit-mask-waves.png) records the
  earlier shared-seam version.

Use these as visual references for one fitted Blender shell and two finishes.
Fit the rim and eye openings to the doll in both the Base cowl and Hermit hat.
Translate the embroidery into clean repeatable texture strips and check it on
the Base waist wrap and Hermit sash or sleeve. Start with full Base, full
Hermit, and a mixed set at gameplay zoom. The swatches are concept art for the
[Mask + Pattern test](../../docs/design/pattern_system.md).
